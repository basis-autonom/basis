The split

worked on $AI / NVDA, 7 days
price_usd       = pool_ratio × stock_price
stock_component = stock_price(t) ÷ stock_price(t−n) − 1  →  218.00 ÷ 173.84 − 1 = +25.4%
meme_component = pool_ratio(t) ÷ pool_ratio(t−n) − 1  →  0.000189 ÷ 0.000169 − 1 = +11.8%
total           = (1 + 0.118) × (1 + 0.254) − 1 = +40.1%

The two components multiply rather than add, because both act on the same price. Adding them would overstate the total on large moves.

Where each number comes from

pool_ratioSwap events on the pool
stock_pricetoken's Chainlink feed
float on chaintotalSupply on the stock token
locked in poolbalanceOf, pool address
market hoursfeed update cadence
corporate actionsUIMultiplierUpdated logs

Two sources only: a public RPC and the feeds already on that chain. No price API, no third-party index, no internal database of our own numbers.

What Basis does not do

Hold fundsno
Route or execute tradesno
Ask for a walletno
Deploy a contractno
Store your queriesno

There is nothing to audit because there is nothing on chain. Basis reads and does arithmetic.

Limits worth knowing before you trust a number

Multi-hop routesa coin with several pools is attributed to its deepest pool only
Thin poolsunder $10K liquidity the ratio is noise, so they are hidden by default
Feed cadencestock prices update on a heartbeat, so intraday timing is approximate to the minute
Frozen windowswhile the market is shut the stock component is exactly zero by construction, not by measurement
New poolsa pool younger than the window is measured from its first swap
