What a gap means

While the stock is shut its price is held at the last print. The memecoin side keeps trading, so the pool ratio moves. That gives an implied stock price the pool is quietly quoting — which can sit well away from where the stock actually opens.

At the bell the real price snaps in and the pool is instantly mispriced. The gap column is how far off the pool was. The last column is how long it took arbitrage to close it.
